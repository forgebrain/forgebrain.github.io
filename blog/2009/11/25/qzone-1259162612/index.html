why I choose the symbol of "S&s"?

Nov 25th, 2009

why do I choose the symbol of "S&s"? There are many reasons that can accout for.So I have to select the main reasons to describle .

First of all ,my attention often focused on the beatiful girls when they passed by me .So I could notice their shapes which could remind me of "S".As we know,the shape of the beautiful girls is like "S".Not only the tall beautiful girls but also the litttle pretty girls could make me pay attention so I put them together which can pass the meaning on.

Secondly ,if the symbol is applied to the brand of the paper company ,it is possible to be explained as"Special & soft",But I am not opening a paper company .I am still a small student which can change himself only by chance.So I can't imagine what I am going to be. For this ,I said the meaning of "S"which is deeply rooted in the Chinese culture because its shape is similar to the "bagua"which was dated back to many years ago.But do you know the true meaning of the bagua?why do people invent it:?I couldn't answer it clearly. as dfferent people have their own opinions .In my view,the meaning of "S" is that the future is filled with misteries and you can't say what willl happen.the another explanation is that the human being should obey the natural rules and continue to dig the truth and use it.There are many truths for the human being to dig out so the thinker invent "S"which shows their respect and appreciatiton to the nature or the man who charges everything.As a small student ,I will pretend to respect the nature which means nothing to me but I am willing to do this.

Thirdly ,I will tell you the truth meaning to me.What is most important in our life is our heath because it is the condition that we can enjoy other things. So I explain "S" as "Strength & speed" because Only if we possess them ,we can become the man which we dream of be going to..Living the free compus, we become lazier and lazier.Our heath condition also become weaker and weaker that we can't possess health.So even though we face many diffficulties of today and tomorrow,we do more exercises so we can improve our health and study.I also want to tell the similar meaning of "Scheme &sleeping",In our lives ,we should make a good plan so we we can make good preparation for the furure. for everything,we should obey this rules.we can't study until midnight and have no rest but you have to sleep until noon tomorrow.as a consequence,we should have a good scheme so as soon as the sleeping time arrives, we must go to the bed.It benefits us a lot because it contributes to our health and our study and it makes us achieve our dreams steps by steps.I have the unshakable faith that "S&s" can arouse my inspiration and imagination which I never dream of.

Fourthly, the meaning which concerns the social relationship is Sharing & sinnereness.It is apparent that we live in together and we should have friends which we can share happiness and sorrow with them so that we can release the joy and love which is used to fill the harmony world.But one thing to remember ,I should make friends sinnerely but not causally so when one is in difficulty,the others are willing to help.As a saying goes, a friend in nees is a friend indeed.Sharing & sinnereness faciliate my friendships so I warn myself that I should insist on it for my all life.

As above goes,I have the last meaning and the special meaning.The last meaning is that the road is long and not always straight.So when we is lost in "S" road ,we should have faith that we will be able to hew out of the mountains of depair a stone of hope.nothing can stop us advancing forward.

the last meaning is poisonous.why?Because when you spell the "S",the sound is like the sound of snake.Sometimes life is dangerous,we are possible to be cheated but we have no awareness.If nothing happens ,it is fortune for us .In the oppsite ,if the disaster happens,we are also continuing to figure out the best way to slove problems even though we are faced with the danger of death.Under whatever condition, we should have a dream.

with the symphony of the last words of this passage, I will end up with the words of the great black man.

I have a dream that one day every valley shall be exalted ,every hills and mountain shall be made low,the rough places will be made plain, and the crooked places places will be straight.

This is our hope .With this faith we go to college for study.with this faith we struggle to transform ourselves /with this faith we are continuing to overcome different kinds of diffculties.

beliving we will achieve our dreams one day.

let confidence ring from our heart!

let strength ring from our playground!

let knowledge ring from our classroom!

let skills ring from our labs!

let happiness ring from our accomdation!

but not only that ;let spirit ring from libary!

But the important one to remember :let opportunity and fortune ring for ourselves.

last sound : say hello to "S&s".

Why am I so foolish?

Nov 4th, 2010

After thinking serveral days and talking with others , I began to realize the problems I have.That is why I am so foolish.

1 .not have a willing heart to accept new minds to update myself.

  1. not know how to use the googel or baidu to help me solve my facing problem.

  2. not hungry for knowledge and have no interest .

4.have some problems in character ,for example, not be board minded (心胸广阔)but just small-minded(心胸狭窄);not be willing to get closer to others and be aquainted with them.

  1. be accustomed to the way I formed,and I have no awareness to change it and I have no vigor of innovation.

  2. not do things efficiently .

7 .memories are short and not have a clear and whole mind to summary the knowledge.

  1. not be willing and calmed to learn things by myself but just depending on teachers or classmates.

  2. not learn to how to protect myself and care others.

10. not to follow my heart.

In short , the problem rooted in my close thoughts(固步自封) and my ugly character(小人性格).

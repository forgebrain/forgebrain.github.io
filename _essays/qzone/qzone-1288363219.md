昨天Copy同学的作业，感觉很心酸，我这么一个人，从上“大学”以来，从来就没有独立完成过作业，可能我不具备独立完成作业，独立思考的能力。从工程制图，数学分析，我就开始依赖同学，到后来的大学物理，线性代数，甚至大学物理实验报告，我一直重复着这种劳力运动，而现在学的离散数学，电路基础，大学物理，也逃不过这一劫。

回想过去的日子，上课睡觉，就算不睡觉，听了也等于无听，还是不懂得怎样去完成作业。每当临交作业的时候，还是老一套，反正不会做，不交更不好，于是就进行一种体力的劳动。

做了一年零二个月的体力劳动，脑真是长草了，好似傻子一样，没有逻辑分析能力，说话含糊不清，混乱不堪，达到脑子空空的人生极致。

回想更之前的日子，好像我就独立思考过一样，其实未然，当年高中，初中的时候，不也是老师重复一遍两遍三四遍，山山红叶红满天，把那些所谓的知识塞到我的脑子里，暂时占据了草的地方而已，现在早已是草茫茫的地方了。After my analyse, I am surprised to find that I come back to the level of primary school including my learning skills and my thoughts.

当我什么都不懂的时候，我突然想起身边的老师，我向一位老师问道：“why am I so foolish?

“He gave me a soft smile and said:”I couldn’t give you the answer but you could get it by yourself.”Afterwards, he kept laughing towards the toilet while I had no idea and thought going to the dormitory.

In the lab, my teacher told me seriously that I should communicate with others and shouldn’t keep silence as a tree.

My teammate reminded me that I should complete my work on time and one thing I knew is to say “嗯，好的“，but in fact, I am out of my mind and heart to finish it and to find the excuse to comfort me.I always remember the saying :”better late than never.”

Today, I saw a “big “man who was doing artificial intelligence research and controlling the robot .I adored him from the bottom of my heart. His intelligence and concentration moved me deeply though I had no idea to understand what he did.

So I concluded that though I am foolish, I have some feeling in my heart.

It was when I was a little boy that I heard a saying while one is foolish, nothing could cure him.

But even though I knew the fact, I had no sadness and cry.That’s why? Because I can’t change my cards distributed, but I have the priority to handle them.whether I am clever or foolish is not so important compared to my life.

Even I have no knowledge and ability, I come back to home and become a farmer or I could become a begger.

Facing my foolish mind, I also have a lot of choices.

So I wondered from one place to other place, from primary school to senior shool,from university to the society.

In this journey, I felt guilty wasting a lot of money or my parent’s life savings while I could also enjoy this journey and my foolish life.
